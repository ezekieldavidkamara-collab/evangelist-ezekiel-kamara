About the pastor

Serving God and His people

Evangelist Ezekiel Kamara serves as a pastor at Christ Healing Center International, with a commitment to sharing the Gospel and helping people grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ.

His ministry emphasizes preaching and teaching the Bible, prayer, evangelism, spiritual encouragement, and standing with individuals and families as they seek God. This website is a place to connect with the ministry, find encouragement, and reach out for prayer.

“For we preach not ourselves, but Christ Jesus the Lord.”
— 2 Corinthians 4:5 (KJV)
